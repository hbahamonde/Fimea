Ethical statement
No ethical board approval was required for this type of study. Data collection was organized by the Finnish Medicines Agency, Fimea, in compliance with the national ethical instructions. Permission to use data for the current study was approved by Fimea, Dnro FIMEA/2022/002982. The survey cover letter contained an explanation of the purpose of the study and the handling of the obtained data, and answering the survey was regarded as informed consent to participate. The data were pseudonymized by the data providers, and the researchers had no access to the personal identifiers of the study subjects.
